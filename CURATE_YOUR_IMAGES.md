## 3. Discernment in Action: Ruthless Rejection Notes

The true skill of working with AI is not generation—it is discernment and ruthless rejection. Below are the specific AI generations evaluated and discarded during our curation process:

### Rejection Case 1: The "Holographic Server Room" Hero
* **What AI Generated:** A high-gloss, 3D isometric illustration of floating purple server racks with glowing neon fiber-optic cables, translucent glass cubes, and floating particle clouds.
* **Why It Was Rejected:** Classic **"AI slop."** It was visually loud, generic, and completely disconnected from actual software engineering. It violated our primary design rule: *the frame must never compete with the painting*. A hiring manager looking at that graphic would remember generic neon lighting rather than containerized backend systems.
* **Action Taken:** Binned the graphic entirely. Replaced it with crisp typography over generous whitespace (`#F8FAFC`), leading directly into real TablePlus database evidence.

### Rejection Case 2: The "Abstract Glowing Database Cylinder"
* **What AI Generated:** An abstract metallic database cylinder with glowing turquoise laser rings and floating binary 1s and 0s.
* **Why It Was Rejected:** Decorative noise with zero evidentiary value. It looks like cheap marketing clipart from 2018. It tells the reviewer nothing about whether the candidate understands relational schemas, SQL injection prevention, or Docker volume persistence.
* **Action Taken:** Replaced with an authentic, unedited screenshot of real PostgreSQL queries executing on `localhost:5432`.

### Rejection Case 3: The "Cyberpunk Rim-Lit Avatar"
* **What AI Generated:** A stylized, hyper-dramatic digital portrait with oversaturated teal-and-magenta rim lighting and airbrushed skin.
* **Why It Was Rejected:** Looks uncanny and artificial. Recruiters hire real team members, not video game characters. 
* **Action Taken:** Selected an honest, well-lit real portrait with natural color balance.
