2. Where Real Captures Beat AI Generation

The core insight: The work is the painting; the portfolio is the frame. Real screenshots of database tables and terminal logs build immediate credibility with senior engineering interviewers, whereas generic AI illustrations raise red flags.

3. Discernment in Action: Ruthless Rejection Notes (Graded)

❌ Rejected: 3D Holographic Server Room Hero

What AI Generated: A glossy 3D isometric rendering of floating purple server racks with glowing neon fiber-optic cables and glassmorphism cubes.

Why Rejected: Textbook "AI slop." Visually noisy, generic, and totally disconnected from real software engineering. It competed aggressively with the case studies and screamed "fake demo."

Decision: Dropped entirely. Replaced with clean typography over generous whitespace (#F8FAFC) leading directly to real TablePlus proof.

❌ Rejected: Abstract Glowing Database Cylinder

What AI Generated: A metallic cylinder graphic with glowing laser rings and floating binary digits.

Why Rejected: Pure decorative clutter with zero evidentiary value. It communicates nothing about relational schemas, parameterized SQL safety, or Docker volume persistence.

Decision: Replaced with an unedited, real screenshot of PostgreSQL executing on localhost:5432.

❌ Rejected: Cyberpunk Rim-Lit AI Avatar

What AI Generated: A stylized digital portrait with dramatic teal-and-magenta lighting and airbrushed skin.

Why Rejected: Uncanny and synthetic. Engineering teams hire real human colleagues, not video game avatars.

Decision: Selected an authentic, well-lit real photograph with natural color balance.

4. Pass / Revise Verification

  • Images map to real needs, the work is shown with real captures, not AI stand-ins.
  • Connective diagrams share one consistent style/mood in our palette (#0F172A, #059669).
  • A real photo is used where the subject is the person.
  • Rejection notes demonstrate genuine technical judgment grounded in proof and credibility.
